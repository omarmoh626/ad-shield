Every case ships the seven items listed in `T08-cases-02-03.md` under "What every case must ship".

## Split

**P1 — Codex builds, Claude audits: case 05, contractor offboarding**

- Request from HR or vendor management with the engagement end date, the account, and the list of access approved for removal.
- Closes when: the specified account is disabled; the approved-for-removal access is gone; a new domain sign-in is denied; the object, its primary group, and its audit history are retained.
- Control: a second contractor with a similar name stays enabled with access unchanged.
- Wrong fixes to test: disabling only (access remains); removing a group that is not on the approved list; acting on the similar contractor.
- Alternate route to test: remove access first, then disable.
- Level 3 script includes `-Confirm:$false` on `Remove-ADGroupMember` or explains the prompt.
- Files: `src/tickets/cases/case05.ts`, `tests/tickets/case05.test.ts`.

**P2 — Claude builds, Codex audits: case 04, department transfer, and Tier 1 flow**

- Request: an HR transfer record from Pharmacy to Finance, plus a note from the former manager who spotted lingering access.
- Closes when: the same account (same GUID and SID) is in the Finance OU; a fresh session can read the Finance share and is denied the Pharmacy share; the enterprise-wide role is preserved.
- Moving the account alone leaves a visible unmet criterion. The learner can try the move, test both shares, see the old access still work, and trace the responsible group in the access inspector.
- Control: another pharmacy technician keeps Pharmacy access.
- Wrong fixes to test: OU move alone; deleting and recreating the account; removing the enterprise-wide role.
- Alternate route to test: fix group memberships first, move second.
- The criteria panel shows the fresh sign-in result and notes that an existing session keeps old access until it ends.
- Tier 1 flow: queue order 01 through 05, rank moves from Helpdesk to IAM Analyst when all five are complete, and an end-of-slice notice says Tier 2 and Tier 3 are not built yet (DECISIONS D12).
- Files: `src/tickets/cases/case04.ts`, `src/tickets/cases/index.ts`, `src/ui/inbox/**`, `src/ui/topbar/**`, `tests/tickets/case04.test.ts`, `tests/tickets/tier1-flow.test.ts`.
